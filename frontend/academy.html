OUR METHODOLOGY & VALUES

The 4 Pillars of Daystar Youth Development

We believe great footballers must first be disciplined, educated, and responsible community members.

📚 1. "Education First"

Scholars must attend school regularly and present termly report cards. We coordinate with local Oluko primary and secondary schools to ensure sport never compromises academic success.

⚽ 2. Technical Mastery

Modern training drills focusing on first touch, quick decision making under pressure, and football intelligence. Every child touches the ball hundreds of times per session.

🤝 3. Character & Mentorship

Teaching anti-drug awareness, community service, emotional resilience, teamwork, and Christian ethics under the club's motto: "NEVER GIVE UP".

⬆️ 4. Direct Senior Pathway

Outstanding scholars are promoted directly into the Daystar United FC senior squad competing in the West Nile Regional League, providing a realistic path to professional football.

ONLINE TALENT REGISTRATION

Register Your Child for Academy Trials

Parents and guardians across Oluko Subcounty, Ayivu East, and Arua City can enroll boys and girls aged 8–20 for evaluation trials at Alua Primary School Playground. Applications are directly routed to the Club Technical Desk for review.

By submitting, you agree to have Daystar United FC coaching staff contact you to schedule weekend trials at Alua Playground. Registration is free.

🏟️ Training Facility & Safety Standards

All youth training sessions take place under supervision at Alua Primary School Playground in Oluko Subcounty. First aid personnel and licensed grassroots coaches are on site during all weekend and afternoon drills. Parents are warmly invited to watch all sessions from the touchline!
